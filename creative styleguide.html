01 / The Brief

Rare Blossom

Brand Overhaul Campaign

Product Focus: Transforming the velvety scent notes of Rare Beauty's cult-favorite Floral Peony Blossom Fragrance Layering Balm into a premier, standalone Rare Eau de Parfum. The product elevates scent-layering into an all-day, high-concentration signature fragrance experience.

Creative Core: Grounded in warm, romantic intimacy and natural botanical elegance. The visual direction balances soft coral and petal pink warmth with luxurious gold metallic accents and earthy terracotta depth, evoking an ethereal, sweet, and sultry floral moment.
