01 / The Brief

Rare Blossom

Campaign Concept

Product Focus: Transitioning Rare Beauty's beloved Floral Peony Blossom fragrance layering balm into an elevated, high-concentration Rare Eau de Parfum flagship line that intensifies scent longevity while preserving its signature tactile intimacy.

Creative Core: Capturing a sweet, sultry, and romantic mood that balances warm botanical sensuality with modern editorial elegance, built around an exquisite scent profile of Peony, Freesia, and Musk Blossom.

Target Audience Gen Z & Millennial Luxury
Fragrance Notes Peony, Freesia, Musk Blossom
Campaign Vibe Sweet, Sultry, Romantic
